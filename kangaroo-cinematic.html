Right now when the Golden Hopper lands on top of a train, time slows a little and the camera tilts up. Here are three bigger, more cinematic takes on the same beat. Watch the preview, then pick the one you like best — A, B, or C.

A — HOLLYWOOD HOP

The camera swings 90° to a side angle for the big jump, letterbox bars slide in like a movie, and there's a white flash and a dust ring on impact. Then it settles behind the roo.

B — LOW-ANGLE HERO

Camera drops to the ground and tilts up — kangaroo looks TEN FEET TALL, flying up over the train against the sky. Then it stays low while the roo rides the roof like a king on a chariot.

C — BULLET-TIME

At the top of the jump, time almost freezes and the camera swings 180° around the kangaroo — you see him from the front, the side, the back — like The Matrix. Then whoosh, back to normal.
