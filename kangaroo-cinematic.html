Kangaroo's Big Jump

A smaller, in-world take on the beat when the Golden Hopper lands on top of a train. Camera drifts slightly to the right so you can see several bounces on the roof, then settles back to normal. Compare with the current behavior.

approach

CURRENT

What the game does today — the world slows to about ⅓ speed and the camera lifts up to look down at the roof.

PROPOSED

Camera starts a slow, seamless swing to a near-side profile well before the kangaroo jumps — you see the arc from the side in slow motion, knees tucked at apex, feet extending for the landing. Impact on the roof is deepest slow-mo (0.15×). Then speed ramps back up through two more bounces, and the camera swings quickly back to the regular chase view before the train slides past. Sound is on by default — the Funky Rat vamp plays underneath. Whoosh at jump. At the peak of the arc, a two-note trill (C–B) on real electric guitar samples lands directly on a big sustained A chord (A2 + E3 + A3 + C4 + E4 + C5) — the chord's top C5 IS the trill's resolution. Rings through the descent, landing (kick accent only), and into the bounces. For faster iteration on just the audio, there's a sound lab with one-click triggers for each element.
