Night Drive

someone driving in the rain · a film-score sketch

What you're hearing: the rain, thunder, windshield wipers, string pads and low cello are all synthesized live — no recordings, just shaped noise and oscillators. The piano is the one real instrument: a sampled concert grand (every note an actual recording), streamed free from the Tone.js library. That's the recipe for richness in the browser — synthesis for atmosphere, samples for the instruments that have to sound real. Full sampled string and brass sections work the same way, they're just bigger downloads.
