How to Play

You're a rat on the tracks. Run forever. Don't get squished.

Your Runner

🐀🐿️🦔🦘Pick your character on the start screen: the Rat (classic), the Night Glider (can glide), Rainbow Spike (can dig), or the Golden Hopper (shiny).

Moves

⬅️➡️Swipe (or arrow keys) to switch tracks.
⬆️Swipe up or tap to jump.
⬇️Swipe down to duck — under signs, vines, AND trains. You stay flat until the train has passed.
🐿️Night Glider only: tap again in mid-air to GLIDE over anything jumpable. Tap once more (or duck) to drop. You sink slowly, so don't glide forever!
🦔Rainbow Spike only: swipe down to DIG underground — tunnel under TWO trains in a row before popping up. Swipe down again to surface early.
🦘Golden Hopper only: a BIG hop — clear LOW CLEARANCE signs/vines/arches in one bound, or land right on top of a train and ride it until it's gone.

Watch Out

🚆Trains — switch tracks, duck and squeeze under, or dig beneath them.
🚧Jump these: striped hurdles, fallen logs (jungle), red boulders (outback), giant clams (sea), snowmen (snow).
⚠️Duck these: LOW CLEARANCE signs, hanging vines (jungle), kangaroo signs (outback), coral arches (sea), icicle arches (snow).

Snacks

🧀Cheese — 25 points, everywhere.
🍕🍌🥧🍣🍦The rare glowing treat changes with the land: pizza 100 · banana 60 · meat pie 70 · sushi 80 · ice cream 90.

Power-ups

🧲Magnet — for 8 seconds, cheese from every track flies to you.
⛑️Helmet — you keep it until something hits you; it takes the hit and you keep running.
🚀Rocket — fly over everything for 4 seconds.
⭐Star — double points for 8 seconds.

The World

🌍Every 20 seconds is a new LEVEL in a new land: open country → the Amazon rainforest → the Australian Outback → the Deep Sea → the Snow World → round again, forever.
🐢Each level starts slow and speeds up — and every level begins a little faster than the last one did. The music keeps pace with you.
🤫Secret: add ?level=5 to the web address to start in the Snow World (any level number works).

Scoring & Names

🏁Running scores points — the faster the game gets, the faster they come. Your best score saves to the leaderboard automatically.
🐀Your name is yours: a secret RAT CODE in ⚙️ settings protects it. Use the same name + code on another device to stay you.
🌏Pick your country in ⚙️ settings and your flag flies beside your name on the leaderboard.
⏸️Pause anytime with ⏸ or the P key. ⚙️ has your name, your country flag, rat code, game speed, sounds and the soundtrack picker.
