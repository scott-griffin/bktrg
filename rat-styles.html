Rat Run Styles

For Thomas — all seen from behind, front paws out. Which one?

A — Hopper

Light bouncy hops, paws peeking out at the top of each one. Closest to what the game does now.

B — Scamper

Low to the ground, fast little steps, paws ticking quickly. How a rat sneaks along a wall.

C — Gallop

A real rat run: hind feet push off together, front paws reach way out mid-leap, head dips with each stride.

D — Big Leaper

Cartoon version of the gallop — huge springy leaps, paws stretched out superhero-style, big squash on landing.
