Toon Shading

For Thomas — four cartoon looks for the rat. "Toon shading" means instead of smooth shadows, you get flat bands of color like in a comic book.

A — Normal

What the rat looks like right now — smooth lighting, realistic shading. The reference.

B — Soft Toon

Five flat bands of light instead of smooth shadows. Cartoony but gentle, no inked outlines.

C — Classic Cel

Three strong bands — the Saturday-morning-cartoon look. Clean light, clean shadow, nothing in between.

D — Comic Ink

Two bands and a bold black outline around every piece. Looks drawn in a comic book.
